# COCOON PERMANENT SOCIAL MEDIA VISUAL STANDARD & REFERENCE GUIDE
*Saved on: 2026-11-10 | Status: Strict Permanent Atelier Standard*

## The Golden Aesthetic Rule
**NEVER make corporate flyers, digital catalog banners, colored advertising borders, or cliparts.**
Every single visual asset created for Cocoon (Instagram and Pinterest) MUST strictly match the viral organic lifestyle aesthetic of high-end cottagecore, Korean cafe, and slow-fashion artisanal creators.

---

## 1. Pinterest Reference Standard (Vertical 2:3 - 1000x1500)
Based on direct atelier reference pins:
- **Aesthetic Hamper & Bento Box Packaging**:
  - Brown corrugated kraft gift box or eco-friendly bento box with crinkled shredded kraft paper filling.
  - Tucked handmade pieces: crochet bow hair clip, daisy floral coaster, heart charm, pastel scrunchie, mini twine-tied flower bouquet or KitKat bar.
  - Handwritten mini note card (e.g., "Just for my friend", "Handmade with love", "Made to Order in India").
- **Handheld Lifestyle Moments**:
  - Natural hands holding smartphones with beaded crochet phone wrist straps resting against soft linen sheets.
  - Handheld charms, keychains, claw clips, and fingerless mittens in natural, warm ambient lighting.
- **Sun-Drenched Coffee & Craft Flatlays**:
  - Warm wooden table or desk with a ceramic coffee mug (latte art), ivory milk cotton yarn ball, wooden/rose gold crochet hook, and a delicate scalloped kerchief or granny square piece in progress.
  - Natural golden hour shadows and soft morning sunlight.

---

## 2. Instagram Reference Standard (Square 1:1 or 4:5 - 1080x1350)
Based on direct atelier reference posts:
- **Cozy Bed & Desk Lifestyle Scenes**:
  - MacBook open on a minimalist desk, fluffy ivory milk cotton yarn ball, steaming ceramic mug, hand-knitted kerchief laid out effortlessly.
  - Cozy bedroom duvet/linen with open book, crochet bookmark, yarn basket, and warm fairy lights.
- **Minimalist Aesthetic Flatlays**:
  - Clean, soft rumpled white or oatmeal linen with a single statement piece (e.g. Sage Green Bow Keyring, Chocolate Bow Bag) with subtle, high-end editorial typography or delicate hand-drawn doodle accents.
- **Work-In-Progress & Handcraft Intimacy**:
  - Hands in soft knit loungewear holding a wooden crochet hook, looping pure milk cotton yarn.
  - Macro texture showing genuine, dense stitches and cloud-like softness.
  - Three pastel yarn cakes (burgundy, cream, mocha) resting on a rumpled linen blanket.

---

## 3. Mandatory Color Palette & Mood
- **Base Backgrounds**: Warm oat milk, natural kraft paper, rumpled white linen, warm honey oak wood, soft beige duvet.
- **Yarn Accents**: Milk cotton cream, sage green, blush pink, rich terracotta, chocolate brown, vintage burgundy, buttercup yellow.
- **Atmosphere**: Warm daylight, golden hour glow, cozy indoor sanctuary, calm slow-fashion morning.
